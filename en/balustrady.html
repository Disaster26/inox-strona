How to order a railing

You do not need to know any system names. A photo, dimensions and the location are enough; we settle the rest on site.

  1. 1

    Free, no-obligation quote

    Send us the location, a photo of the installation spot, dimensions and the railing type. We will reply with a preliminary quote.

  2. 2

    Site visit

    We discuss the details and check dimensions and installation options. Based on that we prepare the final quote.

  3. 3

    Execution

    Once you accept the quote, we prepare the shop drawings, make the railing in our workshop, deliver it and install it.

Welded railing

Welded in our workshop, installed on site · 5 photos

We weld the railing as a whole or in sections in our workshop and then install it on site. We weld using the TIG method under argon shielding.

Welded railings can be powder coated in a colour from the RAL K7 chart. We paint stainless steel, so even if the paint layer gets damaged, the railing will not start to rust.

More about glass

Glass types

VSG

Laminated

Two panes bonded with a special interlayer. When broken, the glass does not fall apart and the panel stays in the structure.

ESG

Toughened

Much stronger thanks to heat treatment and resistant to thermal shock. When broken, it shatters into blunt pieces.

VSG+ESG

Toughened and laminated

Two toughened panes bonded with an interlayer. Used mainly in all-glass railings and with point fixings.

Colours

  • Standard clear float glass, or optiwhite (low-iron) glass if needed.
  • Body-tinted glass in bronze or black.
  • We vary the colour intensity by combining a clear pane with a tinted one, or two tinted panes. A matt interlayer intensifies the colour and removes transparency.

Thicknesses

  • 8mmtwo 4 mm panes and interlayer

  • 12mmtwo 6 mm panes and interlayer

  • 16+mmtwo 8 mm panes and interlayer, or thicker
    all-glass railings on point fixings or linear profiles

What the price of a railing depends on

We do not publish a price list because there are too many variables. The price depends on:

  • Railing type
  • Number and kind of individual parts
  • Size of the job
  • Installation conditions: layout, access, substrate
  • Distance from our base: nearby we do not charge for travel; for more distant places we add the travel cost to the quote, based on how many people and trips are needed (service area)

What the price includes

  1. Shop drawings tailored to your expectations. A drawing for manufacture, not a visualisation.
  2. Making the railing in our workshop.
  3. Delivery and installation.

All questions and answers

How much does a stainless steel railing cost?

We do not publish a price list because too many things affect the price: railing type, number and kind of parts, size of the job, installation conditions and distance from Ostrów. Send us the location, a photo and dimensions and we will reply with a preliminary quote.

Why are railing prices quoted net?

Because two Polish VAT rates apply: 8% when we install a railing in a private home, and 23% when the buyer is a company.

Is the site visit charged?

No. We meet you on site free of charge, discuss the details, check dimensions and installation options, and then prepare the final quote.

What is the difference between a bolted and a welded railing?

With a bolted railing we fix the posts on site first and then the tube infill. A welded railing is made in one piece or in sections in our workshop and only installed on site. A welded railing can also be powder coated in a RAL colour.

Do you also make barriers for stairs and ramps?

Yes. We make stainless steel barriers, railings and handrails for indoor and outdoor stairs, balconies, terraces, driveways, ramps and loading ramps.

How far from Ostrów do you install railings?

Within about 100 km of Ostrów Wielkopolski: among others in Kalisz, Krotoszyn, Pleszew, Jarocin, Kępno and Ostrzeszów, and for larger jobs also in Leszno, Konin, Sieradz or Wrocław. See the full list under Service area.

Can I see samples before ordering?

Yes. Standard designs are on display at our sales office, Sadowa 26A.

Do you take small jobs?

Yes. We take small and large jobs and adapt to your budget.
