The tube mark

Three stainless tube cross-sections arranged like the tubes in the logo, with the red one on top. The mark works on its own where the full logo won't fit: engraving on products, labels on tubes, stamps, site icon and social profiles.

On the site the three rings stand for our three departments: red - railings for homes, black - industrial work and sales. Tube lines and the Argon colour (named after the welding shielding gas) connect the sections.
