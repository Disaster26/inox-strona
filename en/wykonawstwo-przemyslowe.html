Technology

We weld under argon shielding, with TIG or FIBER laser, also on a rotary positioner. We leak-test when the installation requires it. We cut, also at an angle, on cooled band saws and laser-cut parts.

  • TIG
  • FIBER laser
  • Rotary positioner
  • Leak test

Questions from companies

Common questions before a request for quotation.

Which steel grades do you work with?

Stainless steel grade 304, 316 or another grade specified by the customer. We buy material from trusted suppliers.

Do you issue material certificates?

Yes. We issue material certificates and hold a contractor certificate to EN 1090.

Where do you work?

We build and upgrade small and medium-sized installations in Poland and Germany.

Do you take small orders?

Yes. We handle series and single orders, in long-term or one-off cooperation.

Can you make a part to our drawing?

Yes. We work to customer drawings and specifications or with our design support.
